Since 2019 KULFI CORNER

Kulfi Corner was born from a simple passion — to bring back the authentic taste of Indian kulfi that many of us grew up loving. The rich, dense, creamy kulfi made from slow-cooked full-cream milk. Not the quick-frozen kind. Not the artificial kind. The real kind.

Every kulfi at Kulfi Corner is prepared using traditional recipes. We use pure full-cream milk simmered to the right consistency before blending in authentic Indian flavours — saffron, cardamom, rose, pista, badam and much more.

We never compromise on ingredients. No artificial flavours. No shortcuts. Every kulfi is made fresh and served at its very best — because that is the Kulfi Corner promise.

THE KULFI CORNER METHOD

How We Make
Our Kulfi

From pure ingredients to a perfectly creamy kulfi —
every step done with care, every batch made with tradition.

A FIVE-STEP PROCESS

01
🌾
STEP 01

Select Quality Ingredients

We begin by handpicking the finest ingredients — full-cream milk, premium nuts, real saffron, pure cardamom and natural flavouring agents. Only ingredients that meet our quality standard make it into our kulfi.

02
🥛
STEP 02

Prepare the Milk

Pure full-cream milk is slowly simmered and reduced over low heat until it reaches the perfect thick, rich consistency. This slow cooking process is the heart of authentic kulfi — giving it that dense, creamy texture that sets it apart.

03
🌸
STEP 03

Blend Authentic Flavours

Once the milk base is ready, we blend in our authentic Indian flavours — real saffron strands, hand-ground cardamom, crushed pistachios, fresh rose water, gulkand and more. Each flavour is carefully balanced to stay true to its traditional roots.

04
❄️
STEP 04

Freeze Slowly

The kulfi mixture is carefully poured into moulds and frozen slowly — never rushed. Slow freezing allows the kulfi to develop its characteristic dense and creamy texture, quite different from the lighter, airier ice cream. Good kulfi cannot be hurried.

05
✨
STEP 05

Serve Fresh

Every Kulfi Corner kulfi is served fresh to you — straight from the freezer at just the right moment. We make fresh batches every day so you always receive kulfi at its finest. Because great kulfi deserves to be enjoyed at its very best.
